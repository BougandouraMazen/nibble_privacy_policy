Who we are

Nibble is developed and published by Mazen Bougandoura (“we”, “us”), the data controller for the personal data described here. You can reach us at bmazen85@gmail.com.

This policy covers the Nibble app. It does not cover the app stores you download from, or any website you reach by tapping an advert.

What stays on your device

Nibble saves your game locally, using your phone’s standard app storage. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it identifies you:

  • Which levels you have unlocked and completed, and the stars you earned
  • The level you are currently playing and the marks on its board
  • Your sound and music preferences
  • Counters that space out adverts — how many levels since the last one, and when it was shown

Deleting the app removes all of it. There is no backup on our side to restore from, so uninstalling means starting the campaign again.

Advertising

Nibble is free, and adverts pay for it. We show them between levels, and you can choose to watch one in exchange for a free hint or to skip a level you are stuck on. Adverts never block your progress: declining one always leaves the game fully playable.

Adverts are served by Google AdMob. We do not collect or see this data ourselves — Google and its advertising partners process it to select adverts, measure them, and detect fraud. Depending on your consent, that can include:

DataWhat it is used for
Advertising ID A resettable identifier assigned by your phone, used to tailor and cap adverts. Only used for personalised advertising if you allow it.
IP address Approximate location (typically city or region) and fraud prevention.
Device information Model, operating system version, language, screen size — so the advert fits and works.
App information That the advert was requested by Nibble, and the app version.
Advert interactions Whether an advert was shown, viewed, or tapped, and whether a reward was earned.

Personalised adverts

Where you have agreed to it, Google uses your advertising ID and activity across apps and sites to show adverts likely to interest you, rather than random ones. Where you have not agreed, Nibble requests non‑personalised adverts instead: they are chosen from general context rather than a profile of you, though limited data is still processed to deliver the advert and prevent fraud.

Google’s own handling of this data is described in its Business Data Responsibility page, and the companies that may receive it are listed under Google’s advertising partners. You can adjust Google’s own ad settings at My Ad Center.

On iPhone and iPad

Apple requires a separate permission before an app may use the device’s advertising identifier to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. Nibble shows that system prompt — App Tracking Transparency — on first launch, after the consent form described below. Declining changes nothing about the game: you simply see non‑personalised adverts. You can change your answer at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

What you will not see

Nibble is a cozy game, and the adverts in it should not undercut that. We have blocked whole advert categories at the source, including gambling and betting, dating and relationships, and alcohol. Google still decides which advert fills the slot, so we cannot promise perfection — if something inappropriate appears, tell us and we will block it.

What we do not do

  • We never exchange your data for money.
  • We do not read your contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.
  • Nibble asks for no runtime permissions beyond network access to load adverts and to report the anonymous usage described below.

Analytics

Nibble uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is actually played, so that puzzles that are quietly driving people away can be found and fixed. It is switched off until you have answered the consent form described below, and it stays off if you decline.

What is reported is about the game, not about you: which level was started, how long it took, how many stars it earned, whether a hint was used, and which level was closed without being finished. It carries a randomly generated app instance identifier, your device model, operating system version, language and country, so we can tell one session apart from another and see where players are. We never attach your name, email address, contacts, or any account to it, and we never try to identify you from it.

Because adverts pay for the game, we also receive an estimate of what each advert shown to you earned. This tells us whether the game supports itself. It is an estimate produced by Google, not a payment record, and it is not linked to anything you did outside Nibble.

You can find Google’s description of this processing in the Firebase privacy documentation. Uninstalling the app stops all of it.

Europe, the UK & Switzerland

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, Nibble shows you a consent form the first time you open it, before any advert is requested. The form is served by Google’s User Messaging Platform, a Google-certified consent management platform operating under the IAB Transparency & Consent Framework. It is where you choose whether adverts may be personalised and which advertising partners may process your data.

Nothing is requested until you have answered. Consent is genuinely optional: if you decline, the game runs exactly the same — with non‑personalised adverts, or with no adverts at all. No feature, level, or hint is ever withheld because of your answer.

Changing your mind. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone’s settings — on Android under Settings → Privacy → Ads, and on iPhone or iPad by turning off Allow Apps to Request to Track under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Either stops personalised advertising in every app on the device. To revisit the in-app consent form itself, clear Nibble’s app data or reinstall the app; it will appear again on next launch. You can also withdraw consent by writing to us at the address below, and we will confirm it.

United States

If you are in California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia, or another US state with a comprehensive privacy law, Nibble shows you a privacy notice on first launch, served by the same User Messaging Platform, with a control to opt out.

We want to be straightforward about one point. We do not exchange your data for money, but when adverts are personalised, an identifier from your device is shared with Google and its advertising partners for cross-context behavioural advertising. Under California’s CCPA and CPRA, and under similar laws in other states, that sharing counts as a “sale” or “share” of personal information — so we treat it as one and give you the right to stop it.

How to opt out of sale or sharing

  • Choose the opt-out shown in Nibble’s first-launch privacy notice.
  • On Android, turn on Delete advertising ID in your settings. On iPhone or iPad, turn off Allow Apps to Request to Track under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Either stops personalised advertising in every app on the device.
  • Email bmazen85@gmail.com with the subject “Do Not Sell or Share”, and we will apply it.

The categories involved are identifiers (advertising ID, IP address), internet activity (advert interactions), and coarse location inferred from IP. We do not collect sensitive personal information, and we do not knowingly sell or share the personal information of anyone under 16. We will not treat you differently for opting out.

Why we may use it

For players in the EEA and UK, our legal bases under the GDPR are:

  • Consent — for personalised advertising and the storing of, or access to, information on your device for that purpose. You may withdraw it at any time, as described above.
  • Legitimate interests — for showing non‑personalised adverts, capping how often you see them, and preventing advertising fraud, so that a free game remains sustainable.
  • Consent — for the analytics described above. Nibble does not report anything until you have answered the consent form, and reports nothing at all if you decline.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data held about you, correct it, delete it, object to or restrict its processing, withdraw consent, or receive a copy of it. Residents of California and other US states with privacy laws have comparable rights, including the right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information — set out in United States above — and the right not to be discriminated against for exercising them.

Because Nibble holds nothing about you on any server of ours, the fastest routes are usually direct: delete the app to remove everything stored locally, and use Google My Ad Center or your device’s advertising settings for advertising data held by Google. We will also help with any request sent to bmazen85@gmail.com, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Nibble is a general-audience puzzle game. It is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age where you live), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

How long data is kept

Game data stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the app. Advertising data is retained by Google under its own retention schedules, described in the Google Privacy Policy. Analytics events are retained by Google for a maximum of 14 months, after which they are deleted automatically. We hold no copy of any of it.

Security

Game data is stored in your app’s private storage, which your operating system keeps separate from other apps. Advert requests travel over encrypted connections. No system is perfectly secure, but because we operate no servers and store nothing about you, there is very little to lose.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Nibble handles data — a new advertising partner, a new feature that collects something — we will update this page and change the date at the top. Where the change is significant, we will ask for your consent again inside the app before it takes effect.

Support

Nibble is made by one person, and support is a real inbox rather than a ticket system. Write to bmazen85@gmail.com with what happened and which device you are on, and you will get a reply — usually within a couple of days, and always within 30. Bug reports and stuck levels are all welcome.

Several things are faster to fix yourself:

How do I get more seeds? By playing. Seeds are earned by completing levels and are spent only on cosmetic items — scenes, coats and sound packs. Nibble sells nothing: there are no in-app purchases and nothing in the game can be bought with money.

Your progress is gone. Progress is stored only on the device, never on a server, so uninstalling the app or clearing its data erases it permanently and it cannot be recovered.

A level looks impossible. Every board in Nibble is machine-proved to have exactly one solution reachable by reasoning alone, so no level ever requires a guess. Tap Hint for the next step of the logic. If you are convinced a board is genuinely broken, send the level number and it will be checked.

An advert was inappropriate. Gambling, dating and alcohol categories are blocked at the source, but Google chooses which advert fills each slot. Send a screenshot and the advertiser will be blocked too.

You want to change your advertising choices. See Europe, the UK & Switzerland and United States above.
